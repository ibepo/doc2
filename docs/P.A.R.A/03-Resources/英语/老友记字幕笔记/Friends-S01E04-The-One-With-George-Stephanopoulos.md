## 场景 2 - 咖啡馆讨论 (03:00)

### [00:03:00,755 - 00:05:15,543]

**Joey**: So I think we can do better than that.

**Chandler**: Oh, yeah? How?

**Joey**: Well, he was like, "I'd really like to thank everyone for..."

**Joey**: "...for making me feel so welcome in this city."

**Chandler**: That's beautiful.

**Joey**: Then he gave us a list.

**Chandler**: A list?

**Joey**: Yeah, of things we could do to make ourselves more comfortable.

**Chandler**: And?

**Joey**: And there were three items: One, don't watch his news.

**Chandler**: Okay. We could do that.

**Joey**: Two, don't mention his name.

**Chandler**: Fair enough.

**Joey**: Three, don't stare at his hair.

**Chandler**: Joey, that's like half his face.

**Joey**: But the point is, we made him feel welcome.

**Rachel**: I think he's great.

**Joey**: Yeah, that's why he gave us the list.

> [!tip] 学习建议
> - `make someone feel welcome`: 让某人感到受欢迎
> - 乔伊的幽默总是直率但善良

**Ross**: We could stay here and wait for the pizza.

**Chandler**: We did that last night.

**Ross**: We could stay here and wait for the pizza...and wait for the George.

**Phoebe**: I'm gonna go call him.

**Monica**: No!

**Phoebe**: Why not?

**Monica**: Because you're not the type of person who goes around calling people you don't know.

**Phoebe**: Yes, I am.

## 习语与表达

- **go out with**: 与...约会
  - "We went out. We sat down."
- **make someone feel welcome**: 让某人感到受欢迎
  - "make him feel so welcome in this city"
- **be done with**: 和某人分手，结束关系
  - "I'm done with you."
- **be like**: 像...一样
  - "she's a lot like her mother"
