# 英语知识点总结 - Lesson 8: Li Ming Meets Jenny's Class

## 1. 核心对话句型

### 1.1 自我介绍与问候
- **问候语**：
  - "How are you?" - "Very well, thanks!"
  - "Nice to meet you."

### 1.2 询问来源地
- **问句**：Where are you from?
- **答句**：I am from [地点].
- **例句**：
  - I'm from Shijiazhuang.
  - I am from China.

### 1.3 介绍他人
- **句型**：He/She is from [地点].
- **例句**：
  - He is from Shijiazhuang.
  - She is from Xi'an.

## 2. 学校相关词汇

### 2.2 学校时间安排
- **上午**：a.m. (ante meridiem)
- **下午**：p.m. (post meridiem)
- **课程安排**：
  - Three classes in the morning
  - Two in the afternoon
  - We have lunch at school

## 3. 重要句型结构

### 3.1 询问课程数量
- **问句**：How many classes do you have each school day?
- **答句**：We have [数字] classes (each school day).
- **例句**：We have seven classes (each school day).

### 3.2 询问学科
- **问句**：What subjects do you have in your school?
- **答句**：We have [学科列表].
- **例句**：We have English, math and art.

### 3.3 询问具体日期的课程
- **问句**：What subjects do you have on Tuesday?
- **答句**：[具体课程安排]

## 4. 语法要点

### 4.1 人称代词
- **第三人称单数**：He/She is from...
- **第一人称**：I am from...
- **复数**：We have...

### 4.2 介词用法
- **from**：表示来源地
- **on**：表示具体日期
- **at**：表示地点

### 4.3 疑问词
- **Where**：询问地点
- **How many**：询问数量
- **What**：询问事物
