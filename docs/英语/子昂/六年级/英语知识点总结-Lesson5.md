## 2. 核心句型与表达

### 2.1 现在进行时句型
- **基本结构**：主语 + be动词(am/is/are) + 动词-ing + 其他
- **例句**：
  - Jenny and Danny are watching TV.
  - Jenny is sitting in a chair.
  - Danny is sitting beside her.
  - Mr. and Mrs. Smith are reading newspapers.
  - Lynn and Bob are playing cards.
  - I'm writing a letter.

### 2.2 位置描述
- **Jenny is sitting in a chair.**：Jenny正坐在椅子上。
- **Danny is sitting beside her.**：Danny正坐在她旁边。
- **Lynn and Bob are in front of them.**：Lynn和Bob在他们前面。
- **Bob is sitting in a chair.**：Bob正坐在椅子上。
- **Lynn is sitting beside him.**：Lynn正坐在他旁边。
- **I'm sitting at a small table.**：我正坐在小桌子旁。

### 2.3 询问与回答
- **What are we doing?**：我们在做什么？
- **Do you like this show, Danny?**：Danny，你喜欢这个节目吗？
- **Yes, I do!**：是的，我喜欢！

## 3. 语法要点

### 3.1 现在进行时 (Present Continuous)
- **构成**：主语 + be动词(am/is/are) + 动词-ing + 其他
- **用法**：表示现在正在进行的动作
- **be动词选择**：
  - I am + 动词-ing
  - He/She/It is + 动词-ing
  - We/You/They are + 动词-ing

### 3.2 人称代词 (Personal Pronouns)
| 主格 (Subjective) | 宾格 (Objective) | 中文 |
|-------------------|------------------|------|
| I | me | 我 |
| you | you | 你/你们 |
| he | him | 他 |
| she | her | 她 |
| it | it | 它 |
| we | us | 我们 |
| you | you | 你/你们 |
| they | them | 他们/她们/它们 |

### 3.3 人称代词使用规则
- **主格**：作主语
  - 例句：I am writing. He is reading.
- **宾格**：作宾语（动词后、介词后）
  - 例句：Look at me. Give it to him.
