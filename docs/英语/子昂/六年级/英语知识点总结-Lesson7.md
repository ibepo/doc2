# 英语知识点总结 - Lesson 7: On the School Bus

## 1. 核心对话句型

### 1.1 天气描述
- **句型**：It's rainy today.
- **表达**：I always have my umbrella on a rainy day.
- **例句**：
  - It's rainy today.
  - I always have my umbrella on a rainy day.

### 1.2 交通方式
- **句型**：go to school by [交通工具]
- **例句**：
  - Li Ming and I go to school by bus.
  - Sometimes I ride my bike.
  - I never walk to school.

### 1.3 问候语
- **司机问候**：Good morning!
- **学生回应**：Good morning!

## 2. 频率副词

### 2.1 频率副词等级
| 频率副词 | 百分比 | 中文含义 | 例句 |
|----------|--------|----------|------|
| always | 100% | 总是 | I always have my umbrella. |
| often | 60-70% | 经常 | I often go to school by bus. |
| sometimes | 30-40% | 有时 | Sometimes I ride my bike. |
| never | 0% | 从不 | I never walk to school. |

### 2.2 频率副词位置
- **规则1**：放在助动词和情态动词之后
- **规则2**：放在实义动词之前
- **例句**：
  - I always have breakfast. (实义动词前)
  - I often go to school by bus. (实义动词前)

## 3. 交通工具词汇

### 3.1 交通工具
- **bus** - 公交车
- **bike** - 自行车
- **walk** - 步行

### 3.2 交通相关词汇
- **bus stop** - 公交站
- **school bus** - 校车
- **bus driver** - 公交车司机

## 4. 天气相关词汇

### 4.1 天气描述
- **rainy** - 下雨的
- **rain** - 雨
- **wet** - 湿的
- **dry** - 干的

### 4.2 雨具词汇
- **umbrella** - 雨伞
- **boots** - 雨靴

## 5. 歌曲内容

### 5.1 歌曲歌词
- "The rain is falling from the sky."
- "The rain is wet, but I am dry."
- "I like a rainy day. Do you?"
- "I see the bus stop where we meet,"
- "The school bus coming down the street."
- "The driver says, 'Good morning! Hi!"
- "The rain is wet, but we are dry!"
- "And now the rain is gone and done."
- "So good-bye rain and hello sun!"

### 5.2 歌曲重点词汇
- **falling** - 落下
- **meet** - 见面
- **coming down** - 驶来
- **gone and done** - 结束

## 6. 称谓用法

### 6.1 称谓对比
| 称谓 | 中文 | 使用情况 |
|------|------|----------|
| Miss | 小姐 | 未婚女性 |
| Mrs | 太太 | 已婚女性 |
| Mr | 先生 | 男性（已婚/未婚） |
| Ms | 女士 | 女性（婚姻状况不明） |

### 6.2 称谓例句
- This is our bus driver, Ms. Scott.
- Good morning, Mr. Wood!

## 7. 练习要点

### 7.1 填空练习
- Jenny always has her _____ (umbrella)
- _____ Scott is the school bus _____ (Ms, driver)

### 7.2 频率副词练习
- **表格填写**：根据一周活动填写频率副词
- **句型练习**：
  - I always have breakfast.
  - I often read books.
  - I sometimes plant flowers.
  - I never help my mum.

### 7.3 对话练习
- **询问交通方式**：
  - Do you always go to school by bus?
  - Do you walk to school?
- **回答交通方式**：
  - No, sometimes I ride my bike.
  - No, I never walk. I live too far from my school.

## 8. 手写笔记要点

### 8.1 频率副词位置规则
- **助动词/情态动词后**：I can always do it.
- **实义动词前**：I always do it.

### 8.2 重要句型
- **询问频率**：Do you always/often/sometimes/never...?
- **回答频率**：Yes, I always/No, I never...

### 8.3 交通方式表达
- **by + 交通工具**：by bus, by bike
- **动词形式**：ride my bike, walk to school

### 8.4 距离表达
- **too far from**：距离太远
- **例句**：I live too far from my school.
